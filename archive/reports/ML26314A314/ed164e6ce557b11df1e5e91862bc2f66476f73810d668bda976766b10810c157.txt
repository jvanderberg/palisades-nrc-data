                                           SUMMARY

The U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) continued monitoring the licensee’s
performance by conducting a security baseline inspection at Palisades Nuclear Plant,
in accordance with the Reactor Oversight Process. The Reactor Oversight Process is the
NRC’s program for overseeing the safe operation of commercial nuclear power reactors.
Refer to https://www.nrc.gov/reactors/operating/oversight.html for more information.

                                List of Findings and Violations

No findings or violations of more than minor significance were identified.

                                   Additional Tracking Items

None.

INSPECTION SCOPES

Inspections were conducted using the appropriate portions of the inspection procedures (IPs)
in effect at the beginning of the inspection unless otherwise noted. Currently approved IPs with
their attached revision histories are located on the public website at http://www.nrc.gov/reading-
rm/doc-collections/insp-manual/inspection-procedure/index.html. Samples were declared
complete when the IP requirements most appropriate to the inspection activity were met
consistent with Inspection Manual Chapter (IMC) 2201, “Security Inspection Program for
Commercial Nuclear Power Reactors.” The inspectors reviewed selected procedures and
records, observed activities, and interviewed personnel to assess licensee performance and
compliance with Commission rules and regulations, license conditions, site procedures, and
standards.

SAFEGUARDS

71130.16 - Security Performance

Security Performance (IP Section 03.01) (3 Samples)

The inspectors evaluated security performance during observations of the following activities:
     (1)   Access Control

               •   The inspectors evaluated the licensee’s implementation of access controls
                   through observation of personnel, material, and vehicle searches.
               •   The inspectors evaluated the licensee’s implementation of access controls
                   through observation of access control points and control measures.
               •   The inspectors evaluated the licensee’s information protection and Safeguards
                   Information controls.

    (2)    Equipment Testing and Maintenance

           The inspectors evaluated the licensee’s security equipment testing and maintenance
           program through review and observation of records and testing of the following
           systems:
                     1. Alarm station detection and assessment equipment (e.g., closed circuit
                         television, video capture, alarm console)
                     2. Special purpose detection equipment (i.e., metal detector, explosive
                         detector, x-ray)
                     3. Security Weapons
                     4. Physical Barriers (e.g., door, turnstile, biometric reader testing)
                     5. Surveillance, observation, and monitoring as identified in the PSP

               •   The inspectors evaluated the licensee’s perimeter intrusion detection and
                   assessment system testing and maintenance program through review and
                   observation of records and testing.


    (3)    Access Authorization and Fitness for Duty

               •   The inspectors evaluated the licensee’s access authorization records for
                   personnel granted unescorted access to Palisades NGP.

                •   The inspectors evaluated the licensee’s fitness for duty records for personnel
                    granted unescorted access to Palisades NGP.
                •   The inspectors evaluated the licensee’s access authorization and fitness for
                    duty program design and implementation.
                •   The inspectors evaluated the licensee’s fitness for duty sample collection
                    implementation at Palisades NGP.

Problem Identification and Resolution (IP Section 03.02) (1 Sample)

The inspectors reviewed the implementation of the problem identification and resolution
program to identify and correct deficiencies in security risk-significant items.

       (1)   The inspectors reviewed the licensee’s entries in the problem identification and
             resolution program and security event log covering the period from 6/1/2025 to
             10/15/2026.

             The inspectors reviewed a selection of security program reviews conducted from
             10/4/2024 to 12/23/2025.
