SOURCE & METHOD

Nightly archive

At 08:17 UTC each night, fixed code fetches NRC’s plant report index, operating inspection-report index and current-quarter feed. Newly discovered reports are added; earlier reports and findings are retained. Source PDFs and extracted text are saved with SHA-256 hashes.

Fixed parsing rules extract NRC’s finding tables, explicitly labeled minor and licensee-identified violations, and finding statements in public cover letters and executive summaries. The page is rendered from the saved archive and a fixed template. No AI or generated summaries are used. Full report text is available whether or not an entry is extracted.

Dates are report publication dates. Counts describe the indexed public sources, not an all-time or open-violation total. The source inventory is in the repository and can be extended. PDF text extraction preserves wording but can alter spacing and table layout; original PDFs are linked.
